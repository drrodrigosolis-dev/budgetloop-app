BudgetLoop support

Questions, a bug, or something confusing? Write to looptecklabs@gmail.com. It helps to say your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing. Please do not send screenshots that show private financial details.

Sync and iCloud

How do I sync between my own devices?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, make sure iCloud is on for BudgetLoop (Settings, your name, iCloud), and open the app on both. Changes travel through your own iCloud space. It can take a moment, and both devices need internet.

Do I need iCloud?

No. Without iCloud, BudgetLoop works as a normal one-phone app. You only need iCloud to sync devices or to share a household.

I turned iCloud off, or signed out. Did I lose my data?

No. Your data stays on the phone and BudgetLoop does not delete it. Syncing stops and the app tells you. Sign back in to the same account to carry on. If you switch to a different Apple Account, the app keeps what is on the phone, offers you a backup, and asks what you want to do.

Changes from another person are slow to show up.

Check that both phones are online and signed in to iCloud, and that Background App Refresh and notifications are not blocked for BudgetLoop. Opening the app usually pulls the latest changes.

Sharing a household

How do I share with someone?

Open Settings in BudgetLoop, then People, then Invite. Pick the person in the iCloud sharing sheet and send the invitation. They open it on their iPhone and tap Join. Only the people you invite can join.

What can everyone do?

Everyone in the household can read and add, and every item shows who added it. Only the owner can invite or remove people.

What happens if I leave?

You keep a read-only copy of what was there. Nothing is deleted. When you are ready to start your own household, tap Start my own household. The app makes a backup first.

Can the owner leave?

No. The owner can stop sharing instead. Everyone is removed and keeps a read-only copy.

I was removed, or the household ended.

Your phone keeps a read-only copy. You can look at it, take a backup, or start your own household.

I joined a household, but I already had my own data in iCloud.

Your earlier iCloud copy is kept. A backup of your own data is made before you join. The earlier copy is only deleted if you choose to, in a separate step that tells you what will be deleted and offers a backup first.

An invitation link opens the App Store.

BudgetLoop is not installed on that phone yet. Install it, then open the invitation link again.

Backups

How do I back up my data?

Settings, Data, then back up. Choose a folder in Files. The backup holds everything, including wishlist photos. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

How do I restore?

Settings, Data, then restore, and pick the backup folder. The app checks it before touching anything. In a shared household, only the owner can restore, because it reaches everyone, and the app asks you to confirm.

Other questions

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. It is optional, runs on the phone, and only makes drafts that you review. Everything works without it.

How do I lock the app?

Turn on the lock in Settings. It uses Face ID, or your passcode if Face ID is not available.

How do I delete my data?

Delete anything inside the app, or delete the app to remove the data on the phone. To remove the copy in iCloud: on your iPhone open Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, BudgetLoop, then Delete Data. If you share a household and are the owner, tap Stop sharing first, because this deletes the household for you.
