Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-15

In short: BudgetLoop collects nothing. We run no servers and have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We cannot see your data and we do not sell or share it. If you choose to share a household with people you live with, that sharing happens through Apple's iCloud, not through us.

What BudgetLoop stores

Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, wishlist items and photos, tasks, and the names and pictures of the people in your household. This stays on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for BudgetLoop, it is also kept in your own iCloud space so your devices stay in sync. That storage belongs to you and is run by Apple under Apple's privacy terms. The developer has no access to it.

Small settings (theme, language, reminders, saved searches, whether you have seen the tour) are stored on your device only.

Sharing a household (optional)

You can share a household with people you live with, each using their own Apple Account. You start it and you choose who is invited, through iCloud's sharing sheet. Only the people you invite can join.

  • Everyone in the household can read and add, and sees who added what. Names and pictures come from each person's iCloud and can be changed in the app.
  • The shared data lives in the owner's iCloud space and counts against the owner's iCloud storage. Each person's iPhone keeps a copy.
  • Someone who leaves, or is removed, keeps a read-only copy of what was there. Nothing is deleted behind their back.
  • The owner can stop sharing at any time. Everyone then keeps a read-only copy.
  • If you join a household and you already had an earlier copy of your own data in your iCloud, that copy is kept. It is only deleted if you choose to, in a separate step that explains what will be deleted and offers a backup first.

BudgetLoop has no server of its own: we do not receive, store or see the household's data, the invitations, or who is in a household. Apple handles invitations and sharing under Apple's privacy terms.

What BudgetLoop never does

  • No account or sign-in of our own.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting services or third-party code.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data by us.

Network use

BudgetLoop does not open web pages and contains no code that talks to our own or third-party servers. Its only network use goes to Apple: iCloud sync and sharing, done by Apple's iCloud technology (CloudKit) and only when iCloud is on, and App Store purchases, done by Apple's App Store.

Purchases

BudgetLoop is free to download. BudgetLoop Premium (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, renewals, refunds and Family Sharing. We never receive your card or payment details, or who you are: the app only gets Apple's confirmation, on your device, that Premium is active. If you share a household, a small "household has Premium" flag is kept with the household in your own iCloud, so everyone in it gets Premium; it holds no payment information. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

On-device AI and receipts

If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, BudgetLoop may use Apple's on-device model to suggest drafts, and you always review them. It runs on your phone. BudgetLoop does not use cloud AI. Receipts you scan are read on your phone, and you check the result before anything is saved.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera: to scan a receipt, only when you ask.
  • Photos: when you choose pictures, you pick them in Apple's picker. BudgetLoop sees only the ones you select.
  • Face ID: to lock the app, if you turn the lock on. BudgetLoop never sees your face data; iOS only tells it whether you passed.
  • Notifications: reminders for bills and budget limits are made on your phone. In a shared household, Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) sends your iPhone a silent signal when shared data changes, so BudgetLoop can sync and, if you allow it, show a task notification (for example "Ana assigned you: Fix tap"). The notification is made on your phone; we have no server of our own and see none of it.

You can change any of these in Settings, BudgetLoop.

Backups

You can save a backup of everything to a folder you choose in the Files app. A backup is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Children

BudgetLoop is a general household app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices and deleting data

You can export everything, and delete anything, inside the app. Deleting the app removes the data on the phone. To remove the copy in iCloud, see Support.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
