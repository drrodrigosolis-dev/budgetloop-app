BudgetLoop

A calm, private home for your household's money and chores, on iPhone. Your budget, your wishlist and your task board live together, so what you plan to buy and what you actually spent never drift apart. Use it on your own, or share a household with the people you live with, each using their own Apple Account.

Private by design: no account of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud.

What it does

  • Budget: add spending and income in a few taps, scan a receipt, set recurring bills, budgets and goals, and see clear numbers: what you have now, what is still pending, and what to expect over the next 30 days.
  • Wishlist: keep the things you want with a price and a photo; when you buy one, it becomes exactly one real expense.
  • Task board: a simple board for household chores, with categories, due dates and who should do what.
  • Shared household: invite the people you live with through iCloud; everyone reads and adds, and gets a notification when someone assigns them a task.
  • English and Spanish. Free to download; BudgetLoop Premium removes the free limits for the whole household.
